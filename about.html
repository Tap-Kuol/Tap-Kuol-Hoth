About

From Bentiu to Cairo, one step at a time.

My path from South Sudan to software engineering in Egypt.

  • RésuméDownload my CV
  • Beyond codeTours and trips

I am a Software Engineer in the Tech Department at CardoO, an Egyptian smart-devices and IoT company, and a graduate of the Faculty of Computer and Information Sciences at Ain Shams University in Cairo. My journey began in Bentiu, South Sudan.

I started school in 2002 in my mother tongue, Nuer (Thok Naath), and moved to English-language instruction at Yoanyang Primary School in 2004. I continued at Dandok Primary School from 2006 to 2010 and the Seventh-day Adventist Church Primary School in 2011, and completed Primary 8 at Great Upper Nile Primary School in 2012.

In July 2013 I entered Bentiu National Secondary School, but the conflict that began in South Sudan in December 2013 interrupted my studies. I moved to Khartoum, Sudan, in August 2014, resumed my education at Aliab Secondary School in 2015 and earned the Sudan Secondary Certificate in February 2018.

I moved to Cairo in September 2018 and joined Ain Shams University in September 2019. With no computer science in my earlier schooling, I built my programming skills from the ground up. My studies covered software engineering, algorithms and data structures, databases, artificial intelligence, cryptography, network security, cybersecurity and electronics, with programming in C++, C#, Java and Python and a foundation in mathematics and physics. I specialised in Information Systems and graduated on 2 July 2024.

In my first year, I was part of a team of four that built Rover-1, an obstacle-avoiding robotic car programmed in C++. My graduation project, completed in a team of six and graded Excellent, was an AI system that analyses football match videos to track players, score their performance and predict it. I joined CardoO in November 2025.

Studying across three countries has strengthened my ability to adapt quickly, learn continuously and see my commitments through.
